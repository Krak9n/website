Projects


A project is an undertaking on which one dedicates one's leisure time. Building things, writing things, tinkering with things. Doing stuff. It could be anything: a math research paper; participation in an Olympiad; or writing a bootloader in Assembly. I consider anything I spend time on to be a project of some scale.

There is a saying among people of IT industry, not just programmers, that after some time of getting started with programming, you need to find something that interests you the most, something that you can focus on, and work on. It could be a couple of things. But generally, people choose one or two.

I, on the other hand, still haven't figured out what I want to do. For example, I want to grow as a systems administrator because of the stability it offers. However, I am also interested in offensive cybersecurity. Then there is graphics programming, which in itself has so many branches. I think you see the point. I am interested in a lot of things.

As well as everything listed below, I also plan to write a compiler for Plankalkul and work on a couple of other projects. For now, I am mainly exploring the Wayland API and reverse engineering with web security.
